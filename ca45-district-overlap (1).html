On the ballot: State Assembly

All 80 Assembly seats are up every two years. Six districts cover CA-45; Districts 70 and 67 hold nearly four-fifths of its land.

On the ballot: mayors and city councils

City offices are nonpartisan. Fifteen of the 17 cities inside CA-45 have council or mayor races on Nov. 3; only Cerritos and Lakewood do not. Four elect a mayor directly. In cities only partly inside CA-45, each council race is marked by whether its district is inside. The contested council districts in Placentia and Seal Beach, all three contested Santa Ana wards, Fullerton District 5 and Stanton District 1 turn out to lie outside CA-45. Cities are ordered by how many CA-45 residents they hold.

Midway City is unincorporated and has no city council. Council districts are on the map for the Orange County cities. Norwalk, Artesia, Cerritos, Hawaiian Gardens and Fountain Valley elect their councils citywide. Lakewood votes by district, but its district lines are not shown here.
